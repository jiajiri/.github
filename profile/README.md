## What is Jiajiri?

**Jiajiri** (*Swahili for "freelance", "self-employment", or "livelihood"*) is an open-access platform built to eliminate barriers between African talent, worldwide employment opportunities, and cutting-edge academic research.

Traditional employment networks impose pay-to-play paywalls, recruiter subscription fees, and opaque candidate evaluation algorithms. Jiajiri connects Africa's high-caliber workforce with global remote engineering opportunities and open-access academic literature, completely free of recruiter paywalls and subscription gates:

- **Remote Engineering Careers**: Hand-curated, verified remote roles across software engineering, distributed systems, AI/ML, and infrastructure, featuring transparent compensation and zero recruiter spam.
- **Open Academic Preprints**: Direct access to computer science and machine learning research preprints without institutional paywalls or login friction, with one-click citation copying.
- **Privacy-Preserving Career Intelligence (ATS)**: In-memory evaluation and skill-gap feedback designed to assist candidates without collecting, storing, or reselling personal information.
